Practical notes from the field

Better  ductwork  starts  with  better  decisions.

Three short guides explain the details that influence airflow, energy use and the long-term quality of a central air-conditioning installation.

01

GI or aluminium?

GI offers strength for demanding duct runs, while aluminium brings light weight and corrosion resistance. The right choice depends on location, load and environment.

02

Why insulation matters

Correct insulation limits heat gain, reduces condensation risk and helps conditioned air reach each outlet at the intended temperature.

03

Diffusers shape comfort

Sizing and positioning a diffuser correctly helps air mix gently across a room, avoiding drafts, dead zones and unnecessary noise.
